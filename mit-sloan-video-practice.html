How it works

Just like Video Question 2

Click Start and a question appears. You get 10 seconds to think, then 60 seconds to answer out loud. After each question you'll get a quick self-check before the next one. Nothing is recorded or saved. The camera is only there so you can practice looking at the lens.

MIT Sloan's application has two videos. Video Question 1 is the one you prepare: up to 90 seconds on who you are, where you come from, and what's shaped you. Video Question 2 is the surprise: a randomly generated, open-ended question (think "an achievement you're proud of" or "a conflict in a group project"). That's the one this tool is for.

Lisa's reminders

Before you start

  • Only make ONE central point. Don't make multiple points. That will definitely derail your answer.
  • State your point first. Flip the usual sequence where you set a lot of context. Say your conclusion first, then expand on it.
  • 60秒非常宝贵：不要浪费时间讲 situation / context，着重讲你做了什么、改变了什么、影响了谁。
  • 不要念文书。对照着文书 paraphrase，就像跟朋友聊天一样。
  • Don't boil the ocean. 4–5 个不同角度的故事（leadership, inclusivity, failure…）就够了，剩下的靠临场应变。

Tech checklist for the real one

On submission day

  • 用电脑，不要用 iPad 或手机。平台对移动端支持不稳定。
  • 有线网络优先。如果只能用 Wi-Fi，坐到离路由器最近的地方。
  • 光源在你前方，不是背后。
  • 摄像头抬到眼睛高度，可以在笔记本电脑下面垫几本书。
  • 背景干净（比如一面墙），上半身穿正装。
  • 每题录完不要离开电脑。视频在上传，中途离开可能导致失败。
